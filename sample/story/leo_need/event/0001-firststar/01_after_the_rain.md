# 1. First Star After the Rain

Ichika: The rain finally stopped. Look, you can see the first star already.
Saki: My brother walked me here under his umbrella the whole way. Tsukasa never lets me get wet.
Ichika: That sounds exactly like him.
Honami: The puddles are going to be everywhere tomorrow morning.
Shiho: Then wear better shoes.

---

Saki: Sorry for making you all come over. I couldn't manage on my own today.
Ichika: Honami, you're so dependable when things like this happen. It's kind of amazing.
Honami: I have a younger brother, so I'm used to it.
Shiho: ...
Saki: Thank you, Hona.

---

Saki: Hey, do you ever go shopping with your little brother, Honami?
Honami: I'd like it if we went out more together, but he's at an age where he's a little shy about it.
Saki: Aww, that's still cute though!
Ichika: Give him a few years and he'll come around.
